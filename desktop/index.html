                Sync saved posts, which posts All has shown, and the All feed itself with Royal on iOS and in other browsers, through your private iCloud database. Optional: without it, Royal keeps everything in this browser.

                1. Configure CloudKit

                Use the same container and environment as the iOS build. See the setup guide in this extension’s source folder.

                Configure Apple’s sign-in callback to this exact URL:

                Extension origin:

                2. Sign in to iCloud

                Use the Apple Account signed into iCloud on your iPhone. Apple handles the login, and Royal keeps you signed in until you clear its session.

                3. Turn on sync

                Sign in first (step 2). On iOS, turn on Settings › Sync with Desktop with the same Apple Account. iOS syncs saved posts and seen posts; the All feed itself syncs between browsers.

                Sync is off.

                Turning sync on copies this browser’s library to the signed-in Apple Account without overwriting anything already there. Each Apple Account keeps its own copy in this browser.

                Connection troubleshooting

                This checks extension host access and CloudKit reachability with an invalid test token. It does not send your real token or change your sign-in session.

                For a browser network error, open this extension’s Details in the browser’s extensions page, inspect its service worker, then retry Sign in. Note the browser error code; request addresses can contain tokens, so do not share full URLs.
